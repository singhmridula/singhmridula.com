We are hiring: join the ComPASS team

About us

ComPASS (Scalable and Verifiable Positioning) is a research and technology-transfer project at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, developing next-generation secure wireless positioning technologies. Our goal is to make positioning systems more reliable and resistant to manipulation by combining wireless communications, signal processing, embedded systems, and security.

We are building and testing new technologies that can work with existing terrestrial and satellite communication infrastructure, with potential applications in aviation, maritime navigation, autonomous systems, and critical infrastructure.

We are looking for engineers who understand the fundamentals, enjoy solving technical problems, and are motivated to learn and build new technologies.

1. FPGA / Embedded Systems Engineer

We are looking for an engineer interested in digital hardware, embedded systems, and the implementation of wireless communication technologies.

What you’ll work on

  • Develop and implement digital signal-processing components on FPGA and embedded platforms.
  • Work with software-defined radio (SDR) hardware.
  • Implement timing-sensitive communication and signal-processing functions.
  • Integrate hardware and software components.
  • Support hardware testing, debugging, and experimental validation.

What we’re looking for

  • Understanding of digital electronics, embedded systems, and computer architecture.
  • Familiarity with digital logic design and programming concepts.
  • Basic understanding of wireless communication systems.
  • Interest in FPGA development, hardware implementation, and real-time processing.
  • Experience with FPGA tools, VHDL/Verilog, C/C++, or SDR platforms is an advantage, but familiarity with every technology is not required.

2. Signal Processing & Wireless PHY Engineer

We are looking for an engineer who enjoys understanding how wireless signals work and how information can be reliably transmitted and recovered.

What you’ll work on

  • Design and evaluate algorithms for wireless communication and signal processing.
  • Work with signal timing, synchronisation, and wireless propagation effects.
  • Analyse how noise, interference, and channel conditions affect signal reception.
  • Develop simulations and evaluate signal-processing algorithms.
  • Implement and test algorithms using SDR platforms and experimental setups.
  • Investigate how wireless signals can be used for reliable positioning and ranging.

What we’re looking for

  • Good understanding of signals and systems, digital signal processing, and wireless communication fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with concepts such as modulation, filtering, sampling, and synchronisation.
  • Basic knowledge of wireless channels and signal propagation.
  • Ability to develop simulations and analyse experimental results.
  • Experience with MATLAB, Python, C/C++, SDR, or communication system simulation.

3. Satellite Communications Engineer

We are looking for an engineer with a solid foundation in wireless communications who is interested in applying these concepts to satellite systems.

What you’ll work on

  • Study and evaluate wireless communication links between satellites and ground systems.
  • Analyse signal propagation, timing, frequency shifts, and communication delays.
  • Develop simulations and experiments to evaluate satellite communication performance.
  • Work with satellite channel emulators and SDR platforms.
  • Support the development and testing of satellite-assisted positioning technologies.
  • Collaborate with other engineers on system design and experimental validation.

What we’re looking for

  • Good understanding of wireless communication fundamentals.
  • Knowledge of signal propagation, modulation, synchronisation, and communication channels.
  • Understanding of basic link-budget concepts and communication system performance.
  • Interest in satellite communications and applying wireless engineering principles to new environments.
  • Previous experience with satellite communications, GNSS, Doppler compensation, or non-terrestrial networks is an advantage, but not essential for candidates with strong wireless communication fundamentals.

4. Wireless Communications Security / Systems Integration Engineer

We are looking for an engineer who understands how communication systems work and enjoys bringing different hardware and software components together.

What you’ll work on

  • Develop and integrate wireless communication system components.
  • Work with SDR platforms and communication protocols.
  • Build and test end-to-end communication systems.
  • Investigate how wireless communication systems behave under interference, manipulation, and other security threats.
  • Develop experimental setups and conduct system-level testing.
  • Collaborate with signal-processing, embedded systems, and satellite communication engineers.

What we’re looking for

  • Good understanding of wireless communication systems and their fundamental principles.
  • Familiarity with digital communications, communication protocols, and signal processing.
  • Basic programming and software development skills.
  • Interest in communication system security, implementation, and testing.
  • Ability to understand how different components interact within a complete communication system.
  • Experience with SDR, Python, C/C++, Linux, wireless protocols, or communication security is beneficial. Prior experience in specialised positioning security is not required.

What we value across all four positions

We are especially interested in candidates who have:

  • Strong fundamentals: A good understanding of wireless communications, signals and systems, and related engineering concepts.
  • Problem-solving skills: The ability to analyse technical problems and develop practical solutions.
  • Programming skills: Experience with programming or simulation tools such as Python, MATLAB, C/C++, or similar environments.
  • Hands-on interest: Motivation to work with real hardware, software, experiments, and prototypes.
  • Willingness to learn: Curiosity about technologies outside their existing area of expertise.
  • Teamwork: The ability to collaborate across signal processing, communications, embedded systems, and security.

Educational background

A degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Telecommunications, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a closely related technical field. We particularly encourage applications from candidates with strong engineering fundamentals who are motivated to develop expertise in these areas.

Why join ComPASS?

  • Work on meaningful technical challenges: Help develop technologies that improve the trustworthiness of wireless positioning systems.
  • Apply theory in practice: Work across simulation, algorithm development, hardware implementation, and experimental validation.
  • Explore different technologies: Gain experience with SDR, FPGA, wireless communications, satellite systems, and security.
  • Collaborate across disciplines: Join a team combining expertise in signal processing, communication systems, embedded engineering, and cybersecurity.
  • Contribute to innovation: Help take research ideas toward real-world demonstrators and practical applications.
