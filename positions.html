Full-time positions on ComPASS

  • Satellite Communications Engineer. MSc in electrical and computer engineering or an equivalent field, with expertise in satellite communication systems. Practical experience with link budget analysis, Doppler compensation, satellite channel emulation and non-terrestrial networks is essential.
  • Telecom Security and Systems Integration Engineer. MSc in electrical and computer engineering or an equivalent field, with expertise in communication system security and system integration. Practical experience with software-defined radio toolchains, and familiarity with protocols such as ADS-B, AIS or 3GPP NTN is required.

PhD and postdoc positions

I am looking for motivated PhD and postdoc applicants in cyber-physical security. The positions are not project-bound, so candidates can freely choose and work on their preferred research directions within the following focus areas:

  • Secure Positioning, Navigation and Timing. Designing and developing secure ranging and positioning systems, including GNSS, UWB, 5G and LEO satellites.
  • Secure Perception and Communication. Security analysis of perception and navigation sensors for autonomous vehicles, such as LiDAR, radar and INS.
  • Preserving Location Privacy. Analyzing privacy risks of evolving communication protocols, such as LTE and 5G.

To apply for any of these positions, send your CV to singh@cispa.de.
