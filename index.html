I am a tenure-track faculty member at the CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security in Saarbrücken, Germany. My research focuses on securing sensing technologies in autonomous vehicles (LiDAR, radar, and cameras) and designing secure positioning systems (UWB, Wi-Fi, LTE, 5G, and GNSS). Our research on autonomous vehicle security is funded by the BMDV through CYPHER-AV. Our ComPASS project is funded by the BMFTR through the StartUpSecure initiative and focuses on demonstrating the resilience of radio waveforms and positioning protocols.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, Switzerland, under the supervision of Prof. Srdjan Čapkun. During my PhD, I developed physical-layer Message Time of Arrival Codes, which laid the foundation for secure proximity-based access control systems. This technology has contributed to securing passive keyless entry and start systems in several car models. Before my PhD, I co-founded Trishulam while pursuing my master’s degree at IIIT-Delhi, India, and worked as a research engineer at Xerox Research Centre India.
